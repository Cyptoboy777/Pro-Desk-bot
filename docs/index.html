Which signals go with bad outcomes (24h)

precision = share of flagged tokens that went bad. recall = share of bad tokens that were flagged. Sample n = the smaller of the flagged and clear groups. Lift = bad rate if flagged / baseline. p = two-sided Fisher exact, not adjusted for the many signals tested. * = intervals do not overlap. Correlated with each other and with market regime: not causal.
